A school ERP demo can look impressive on a dashboard and still leave the office unsure how to correct a student record or find a fee receipt. The quickest way to compare school management software is to bring a few everyday tasks and ask to see each one from start to finish.

Penport School MS, developed by **Penport India** in Patna, Bihar, brings student and staff records, attendance, fee administration and examinations into one system. Use this checklist in a Penport School MS walkthrough or when evaluating another system.

## Start with one sample student

Ask the presenter to create a sample student, assign a class and section, and update a guardian's phone number. Then ask an office staff member to find that record from the student list. This shows how many steps routine corrections take and whether staff can locate the right student when two names are similar.

Use fictional data or anonymized examples. A sample with a common name, a class change and an updated contact number is more revealing than a perfectly clean record.

## Ask each team to test its own work

| Team | What to show in the demo | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office | Find a student and update a contact detail | Staff can identify the right record and understand which fields they can edit |
| Teachers | Mark class attendance and review a previous day | The correct date and class are clear, and a missed entry can be addressed |
| Accounts | Record a sample fee payment and locate its receipt | The receipt amount and the remaining balance agree |
| School leadership | Open an attendance or examination report | The report covers the intended class and reporting period |

Ask who performs each correction and who checks the result. With Penport School MS, use the demo to see how role-based access applies to those responsibilities. For example, can a teacher handle class attendance while access to fee records stays with authorized staff?

## Discuss what happens in the first month

Before choosing a system, agree on which records need to move first. Current student details, class lists and opening fee balances are a practical starting point for many schools. Ask how your existing register or spreadsheet would be transferred, who will check the migrated records, and what support staff can expect during their first reporting cycle.

After each demo, have the office, teachers and accounts team record which tasks they could complete confidently and which questions remain open. Choose the system that supports the work your school actually needs to do.
