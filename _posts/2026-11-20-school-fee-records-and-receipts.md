At the end of a busy fee collection day, the school office needs to answer three questions. How much was collected? Which receipts account for that amount? What does each family still owe? When the answers live in separate registers, finding a discrepancy can take longer than recording the payment itself.

**Penport School MS**, a school management software product developed by **Penport India**, supports fee records, collections, receipts and dues reporting. The workflow below helps accounts teams agree on what to record and how to review it.

## Define the amount due before taking payments

Start with the fee heads and billing periods your school uses, such as monthly tuition and an annual activity fee. Confirm the opening balance for each student before adding new charges. If concessions apply, record the approved amount and identify who authorized it. A clear opening balance makes it easier to explain later why two students in the same class owe different amounts.

## Record the payment and confirm the receipt

Match each payment to the correct student and fee period. Record the amount, date and payment method, and retain the transaction reference where relevant. Check that the receipt reflects what was actually paid, especially when a family makes a partial payment.

Consider a student who owes ₹2,500 and pays ₹1,500. The receipt should show ₹1,500 received, while the dues record should show ₹1,000 remaining. Use a simple example like this in a Penport School MS demo to see how the accounts team can find both figures when a parent calls.

## Reconcile collections at the end of the day

Compare the day's recorded receipts against the cash count and the payment records available to your school. Review totals by payment method before checking the overall figure. If a payment is recorded under the wrong method, the overall total may still match even though the cash and digital subtotals differ.

Keep a short list of exceptions, such as an unsettled transfer or a receipt awaiting correction, with the person responsible and the expected follow-up date. Review unresolved entries before closing the month.

## Review outstanding dues separately

Collections show what was received during a period. Outstanding dues show what remains payable at a given date. Review both views at month-end so office staff can follow up on the correct balance. Check the reporting date, class filters and any approved adjustments before sharing a summary with school leadership.
