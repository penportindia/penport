Bihar schools can make student data reporting easier by keeping one verified school record and checking it against the instructions for each portal. Start with the applicable reporting period, review admissions and transfers, and have a designated staff member confirm the final figures.

A student changes sections. The class teacher updates the attendance list, the office corrects the admission record, and an older spreadsheet still shows the previous section. When the next government portal update comes due, someone has to work out which version is correct. Multiply that by new admissions, transfers and corrections across several classes, and a simple data entry task becomes a school-wide coordination problem.

For school administrators in Bihar, that work can span **UDISE+, e-Shikshakosh and Gyandeep**, depending on the school's responsibilities. Each portal has its own purpose and instructions. **Penport School MS**, the school management software developed by **Penport India**, helps staff organize their everyday records before they prepare, review and submit information through the appropriate government portal.

## What each government portal is for

| Portal | Official purpose | What the school office can prepare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [UDISE+](https://udiseplus.gov.in/) | The Ministry of Education's platform for collecting school profile, student and teacher data | Review the current enrolment list, class and section assignments, and relevant staff records against the applicable reporting instructions |
| [e-Shikshakosh](https://eshikshakosh.bihar.gov.in/login) | Bihar's education management platform, with modules for school monitoring, teacher records and student progression and performance, as described by the [National e-Governance Division](https://negd.gov.in/isl/Directory/statedata/283) | Check student and class records for the module your school has been asked to update |
| [Gyandeep](https://gyandeep-rte.bihar.gov.in/) | Bihar's Right to Education admission portal, through which eligible families can apply for admission to private schools | Where applicable, compare the official allotment with the school's admission record and confirm whether the allotted student has joined |

School teams should check the applicable circular, reporting period and portal instructions for each submission. A school's internal admission number, for example, should be kept distinct from any identifier the official portal uses.

## Why the workload grows across the school year

The same core information appears in admission registers, attendance lists and online reporting. A spelling correction, a transfer or a section change can leave those records out of step. Different reports may also ask for a count as of a specific date, so today's class total may differ from the figure the school needs to report for an earlier period.

The work is especially difficult when staff pass around multiple versions of a spreadsheet. If a headcount differs, the office may spend more time finding the source of the difference than entering the final figure. A clear process for recording and checking changes gives the school a dependable record to refer to when reporting requests arrive.

## Student data checklist before a government portal submission

1. **Check the request.** Record which portal and module apply, what period the report covers, and who is responsible for submitting it.
2. **Verify the school records first.** Review the relevant class lists. Resolve discrepancies such as duplicate entries, missing students and transfers with the staff members who manage those records.
3. **Cross-check the totals.** Compare the requested figures with the relevant school reports and have another authorized staff member review any differences before submission.
4. **Keep a record of what was submitted.** Use the official portal to complete the submission. Retain the acknowledgement or approved summary, where available, alongside the reporting period and reviewer.

## Common questions from Bihar school administrators

### Why can the student count differ between two reports?

Start by checking the reporting dates and which students each report covers. A later class list may include students admitted after an earlier reporting date. Check admissions, transfers and class changes against the period requested before changing any totals.

### What should the office verify before confirming a Gyandeep admission?

Where the Gyandeep process applies, compare the official allotment with the school's admission record and confirm the student's joining status. Follow the [current Gyandeep instructions](https://gyandeep-rte.bihar.gov.in/) for the portal steps relevant to your school.
